How It Works?

Step 1:

Set up a shared vault in seconds, send an invite link to your group, and define your travel goals or squad budget in one place.

Step 2:

Log shared costs on the go—from fuel to food. Vaulet automatically calculates exact splits, updates live balances, and keeps contribution history transparent so nobody has to guess.

Step 3:

Clear out remaining balances without manual spreadsheets or awkward payment reminders, then drop photos and highlights into your shared timeline to lock in the memory.
